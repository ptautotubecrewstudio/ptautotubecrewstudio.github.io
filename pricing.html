Project Pricing

Every Project Has Its Own Scope

The cost of CGI animation VFX and digital production depends on what the project needs. Share your brief and the studio can review the scope before preparing a quote.

How We Prepare a Quote

What Shapes Project Pricing

These details help define the work and prepare a project-specific estimate.

Creative Scope

The visual direction the project goals and the production work requested.

Production Requirements

The assets animation VFX or other production tasks required for the project.

Final Deliverables

The number of outputs their formats and where the finished work will be used.

Schedule

The target dates review stages and any delivery deadlines.

Project Materials

Available scripts references designs models footage and product information.

Review and Revisions

The feedback process and revision rounds agreed for the project.

What to Include in Your Request

Share whatever information you already have. The studio can discuss the remaining details with you.

  • Project idea and main objective
  • Type of production support needed
  • Estimated duration or number of shots
  • Target delivery date
  • Reference images or links
  • Budget range if you have one

Confirm the scope deliverables schedule fees revision terms and payment arrangements in writing before production begins.
