WHAT INFLUENCES PROJECT COST?

UNDERSTANDING THE INVESTMENT.

Several project-specific factors influence the resources, production effort and final quotation.

01 / COMPLEXITY

Creative & Technical Complexity

The level of creative detail, technical difficulty, animation requirements, visual effects and functionality required.

02 / DURATION

Project Duration

Animation length, video duration, number of scenes, production schedule and total project timeline.

03 / RESOURCES

People & Infrastructure

Production team, specialized expertise, computing resources, equipment, software and infrastructure requirements.

04 / DELIVERABLES

Output Requirements

Final file formats, output resolutions, platform specifications, source files and additional deliverables included in the scope.

05 / REVISIONS

Review & Revisions

Number of agreed review rounds, feedback requirements, revision scope and approval process.

06 / DEADLINES

Delivery Schedule

Project urgency, production dependencies, milestone deadlines and resource scheduling requirements.

WORKING ARRANGEMENTS

FLEXIBLE ENGAGEMENT MODELS.

The appropriate commercial arrangement depends on the nature of the work, expected duration and agreed responsibilities.

MODEL 01 / FIXED SCOPE

Project-Based Pricing

A defined project scope, specified deliverables, agreed timeline and documented commercial terms. Suitable for projects with clearly established requirements.

MODEL 02 / MILESTONES

Milestone-Based Pricing

Payments and project progress structured around agreed production or development milestones. Suitable where the project requires several stages of execution and approval.

MODEL 03 / ONGOING

Retainer-Based Engagement

An ongoing service arrangement with defined responsibilities, recurring deliverables and an agreed service period. Subject to scope and mutual agreement.

MODEL 04 / PRODUCTION

Outsourcing & Co-Production

Project-specific collaboration between studios, production teams and business partners. Commercial responsibilities, resources and deliverables are agreed individually.

COMMERCIAL TRANSPARENCY

CLEAR TERMS. BETTER COLLABORATION.

Each project proposal should clearly identify the approved scope, price, deliverables, payment schedule and applicable conditions.

Payment arrangements may be structured around an agreed advance payment, production milestones or other mutually accepted commercial terms.

Revision limits, additional work, change requests, delivery conditions, intellectual property and source-file handover should be documented in the applicable agreement.

Applicable taxes, additional expenses and currency arrangements should be stated in the formal proposal or agreement.

No project pricing, payment schedule or production commitment is final until accepted through the appropriate written agreement.

PRICING INFORMATION

COMMON QUESTIONS.

Why does the company not display fixed service prices?

Production requirements vary significantly between projects. Animation duration, technical complexity, creative assets, production resources and deliverables can all affect the final cost. We therefore use project-specific quotations instead of presenting misleading fixed prices.

Can I request a customized quotation?

Yes. You can submit your project requirements through our official contact channel. After reviewing the scope, a suitable proposal can be discussed.

Can the project be divided into milestones?

Suitable projects may be structured around production or development milestones. Milestones, approvals and payment conditions must be agreed in the project proposal or contract.

Does the quotation include revisions?

The quotation should specify the agreed review and revision allowance. Additional revisions or changes outside the approved scope may require a separate cost review.

Can international clients request quotations?

We welcome enquiries from local and international clients. Currency, applicable taxes, payment methods and other commercial conditions can be discussed during proposal preparation.

How do I start the quotation process?

Visit our Contact page and share your project description, services required, timeline and available budget information.

Is a quotation the same as a confirmed project agreement?

No. A quotation or proposal outlines proposed commercial terms and scope. A project is confirmed only after the necessary approvals and formal agreements.
