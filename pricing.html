OUR PRICING APPROACH

Clear scope first. Then a considered proposal.

At AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO, quotations are prepared around the actual requirements of each project. A short commercial video, a detailed CGI sequence, an animated production and a digital platform involve different workflows, resources and levels of complexity.

For that reason, this page does not publish generic package prices or unverified starting rates. The commercial proposal should reflect the agreed scope, deliverables, schedule, responsibilities and applicable terms.

WHAT INFLUENCES A QUOTATION

The details behind the project investment.

A suitable quotation starts with a clear understanding of the production requirements. These factors help define the work and resources involved.

01 / SCOPE

Project scope

Objectives, project size, number of scenes or pages, required functions and the responsibilities included in the assignment.

02 / COMPLEXITY

Creative & technical detail

Visual style, animation detail, simulation, CGI requirements, VFX complexity, integrations and specialist production needs.

03 / DURATION

Length & schedule

Runtime, number of shots or outputs, production stages, delivery dates and dependencies that affect the project schedule.

04 / RESOURCES

Production resources

Creative and technical roles, equipment, software, rendering, infrastructure and other resources required for execution.

05 / OUTPUTS

Deliverables & formats

Final versions, aspect ratios, resolutions, languages, platform specifications and other agreed output requirements.

06 / APPROVAL

Reviews & revisions

Milestone reviews, feedback rounds, approval responsibilities and the treatment of additional requests or scope changes.

The final quotation and any applicable taxes, payment schedule, usage rights, revision limits and handover conditions should be stated in the written proposal or project agreement.

COMMERCIAL STRUCTURES

An engagement model suited to the brief.

MODEL 01

Project-based

A defined scope, deliverable list, schedule and commercial proposal for a specific assignment.

MODEL 02

Milestone-based

Work and payments arranged around agreed project stages, review points and approvals.

MODEL 03

Ongoing engagement

Recurring production or development support with agreed capacity, services, reporting and commercial terms.

MODEL 04

Production support & co-production

Defined specialist support or collaborative execution with responsibilities and deliverables documented in advance.

An engagement model is confirmed only after both parties review and agree the scope, availability, commercial conditions and applicable project documentation.

PRICING INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

Why are fixed prices not listed on this page?

Production and digital projects differ in scope, complexity, duration, resources and deliverables. A project-specific quotation is more useful than a generic price that may not reflect the actual requirements.

How can I request a quotation?

Use the quote request page to share the project brief and available details. The requirements can then be reviewed before a proposal is prepared for discussion.

Can a project be divided into milestones?

Where appropriate, the project scope, review stages and payment milestones can be documented in the proposal or agreement. The structure must be agreed by both parties.

What happens when the project scope changes?

Additional work or changed requirements may affect resources, timing and pricing. Any change should be reviewed and agreed in writing before the additional work proceeds.

Are taxes, source files and usage rights included?

The proposal or agreement should identify applicable taxes, deliverables, usage rights, source-file handover conditions and any other relevant commercial terms. These should not be assumed unless they are expressly included.
