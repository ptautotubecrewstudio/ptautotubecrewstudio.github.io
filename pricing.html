Why Don't We Publish Fixed Prices?

Production costs can vary significantly depending on the number of assets, animation duration, visual complexity, revisions, rendering requirements, deadlines and delivery formats. We therefore prepare project-specific quotations rather than using one fixed price for every client.

What Can Affect Your Project Cost?

These are some of the main factors considered when preparing a production quotation.

Project Scope

The overall amount of creative and technical work required for the project.

Visual Complexity

Detailed environments, characters, products, simulations and visual effects can increase production requirements.

Animation Duration

Longer animation sequences generally require additional production, rendering and post-production time.

Deadline

Urgent or accelerated production schedules may require additional production resources.
