01. General Information

AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO is based in Indonesia. For business enquiries and project discussions, please contact our team through the official Contact page .

What types of clients do you work with?

We welcome enquiries from brands, businesses, production companies, filmmakers, creative professionals and potential business partners.

02. Services & Capabilities

What services does your studio offer?

Our listed service areas include 3D animation, CGI and visualization, visual effects (VFX), film and video production, digital platforms and creative content.

Explore our Services page for more information.

Do you work on animated films?

Animation and cinematic storytelling are part of our creative production focus. Project scope, production requirements, creative direction and collaboration arrangements can be discussed individually.

03. Projects & Production

How can I start a project with your studio?

Start by visiting our Contact page and sharing your project requirements.

Include information about your concept, objectives, preferred deliverables, expected timeline and any relevant references.

What information should I provide for a project enquiry?

Useful information includes the project description, target audience, creative references, production requirements, preferred delivery formats, timeline and available budget range.

What is your production workflow?

A typical creative production workflow may include: project briefing, concept development, pre-production, production, post-production, review and final delivery.

The exact workflow depends on the nature and complexity of each project.

How long does a production project take?

Production timelines vary depending on project scope, creative complexity, technical requirements, production resources and review schedules.

A project-specific timeline can be discussed after evaluating the requirements.
