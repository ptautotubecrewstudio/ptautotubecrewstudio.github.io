Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, Answered.

How Can We Help?

If you do not find the answer you are looking for, contact our studio and tell us about your project. We can discuss your specific requirements directly.

General Questions

What does PT Autotube Crew Studio do?
PT Autotube Crew Studio is a visual production studio focused on CGI, 3D animation, VFX and video production. We develop visual content for brands, businesses, productions, products and creative projects.
What type of projects do you accept?
We work on a wide range of projects including product visualization, advertising content, cinematic animation, CGI campaigns, VFX, corporate videos, technology visualization, automotive content and other custom visual productions.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. Projects can be discussed and managed remotely, allowing us to collaborate with clients and creative partners across different locations.
Can you work with an existing creative team?
Yes. We can operate as a full production partner or integrate into an existing creative, advertising, film, design or marketing team depending on the project structure.

Services

What 3D animation services do you provide?
Our 3D animation work can include product animation, cinematic sequences, environment animation, technical visualization, promotional content and custom animation based on the project's requirements.
Do you provide CGI and product visualization?
Yes. CGI can be used to visualize products, concepts, environments, vehicles, technical systems and other subjects where high-quality digital imagery is required.
Do you provide VFX?
Yes. VFX services can include compositing, digital environments, visual effects integration and other post-production requirements depending on the project.
Can you handle complete video production?
Yes. Depending on the scope, a project can include creative development, production, animation, CGI, editing, VFX, sound and final delivery.

Pricing & Quotes

How much does a project cost?
Project pricing depends on factors such as duration, complexity, animation requirements, number of assets, visual quality, VFX requirements, production schedule and final deliverables. We provide project-specific quotations rather than using one fixed price for every project.
How do I request a quotation?
Use our Request a Quote page and provide as much project information as possible. The more detail you provide about your objectives, deliverables and timeline, the more accurately we can evaluate the project.
Can you work with a fixed production budget?
Yes. If you already have a defined budget, include it when submitting your project information. We can then evaluate the scope and determine the most appropriate production approach for that budget.
Is the initial consultation free?
Initial project discussions can be used to understand the creative requirements, scope and production objectives before determining the appropriate quotation and workflow.

Timeline & Production

How long does a project take?
Timelines vary significantly depending on the scope and complexity of the project. A short product animation may require a different schedule from a large cinematic production involving extensive CGI, VFX and post-production.
Can you work with urgent deadlines?
Urgent projects can be evaluated depending on current production capacity, project complexity and the required delivery date. Include your deadline when submitting a quotation request.
Will I receive project updates during production?
The communication and review schedule is agreed according to the project scope. Production milestones can be used to review creative direction, animation, visuals and other important stages.

Revisions & Delivery

Are revisions included?
Revision rounds depend on the project agreement and production scope. The quotation or project agreement should clearly define the included review stages and revision limits.
What file formats can you deliver?
Final delivery formats depend on the intended use. Common deliverables can include high-resolution video files, web-ready videos, social media formats and other agreed production assets.
Can you create different versions for social media?
Yes. Additional aspect ratios, durations and platform-specific versions can be included in the production scope when required.

Collaboration & Confidentiality

Can we sign an NDA?
Confidentiality requirements can be discussed before project information is shared. If your project requires an NDA, mention this when contacting the studio.
Can you work with confidential or unreleased products?
Yes. Confidential projects can be handled according to the agreed confidentiality and information-sharing requirements.
Can we use your work in our marketing?
Usage, portfolio display and promotional rights should be agreed as part of the project arrangement, especially for confidential or unreleased work.
