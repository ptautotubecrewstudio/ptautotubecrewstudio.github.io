01 / STUDIO

Studio overview

What is AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO?

AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO is a visual production studio based in Jakarta, Indonesia. Its creative focus includes 3D animation, CGI, VFX, cinematic production, video production and digital content.

Where is the studio based?

Our studio is based in Jakarta, Indonesia. Contact the team to discuss project requirements and possible working arrangements.

What types of clients do you work with?

We welcome enquiries from brands, businesses, production companies, filmmakers, creative professionals and potential business partners. The suitability of each project or collaboration is considered individually.

02 / CAPABILITIES

Services & capabilities

What services does your studio offer?

Our listed service areas include 3D animation, CGI and visualization, visual effects (VFX), film and video production, digital platforms and creative content. Explore the Services page for more information.

Do you work on animated films?

Animation and cinematic storytelling are part of our creative production focus. Project scope, production requirements, creative direction and collaboration arrangements can be discussed individually.

Can you develop customized creative projects?

Project requirements can be discussed according to the creative concept, production scope, technical requirements and intended deliverables. Each project is evaluated individually before a proposed scope and production plan are agreed.

03 / PRODUCTION

Projects & production

How can I start a project with your studio?

Start by visiting our Contact page and sharing your project requirements. Include information about your concept, objectives, preferred deliverables, expected timeline and any relevant references.

What information should I provide for a project enquiry?

Useful information includes the project description, target audience, creative references, production requirements, preferred delivery formats, timeline and available budget range. You can share additional details during the project discussion.

What is your production workflow?

A typical creative production workflow may include project briefing, concept development, pre-production, production, post-production, review and final delivery. The exact workflow depends on the nature and complexity of each project.

Learn more about the process on our Our Workflow page.

How long does a production project take?

Production timelines vary depending on project scope, creative complexity, technical requirements, production resources and review schedules. A project-specific timeline can be discussed after evaluating the requirements.

04 / COMMERCIALS

Pricing & payments

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on project scope, creative requirements, production complexity, resources and final deliverables. Contact our team with your project details to discuss a suitable proposal.

Do you provide customized quotations?

Project proposals and quotations can be prepared according to the requirements and scope agreed during the project discussion. The details should be reviewed and confirmed before work begins.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms depend on the individual project agreement. The agreed payment schedule, milestones and conditions should be documented before production begins.

Do you accept business partnerships?

How can I explore career opportunities?

06 / COMMUNICATION

Contact & communication

How can I contact AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO?

You can contact our studio through the official Contact page. Share your enquiry, project details or business proposal with our team.

Project requirements, production scope, timelines and commercial terms can vary. Contact the studio with the relevant details so your enquiry can be considered individually.
