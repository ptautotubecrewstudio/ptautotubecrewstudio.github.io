Questions & Answers

How Can We Help?

Here are answers to some of the most common questions about working with our studio.

What services do you provide?

We provide CGI, 3D Animation, Visual Effects (VFX), Motion Graphics, Product Visualization, Architectural Visualization, Medical Animation, Commercial Production and Video Editing.

Which industries do you work with?

We work with advertising agencies, automotive, healthcare, manufacturing, real estate, education, entertainment, technology and consumer brands.

Which software do you use?

Our production pipeline includes Blender, Unreal Engine 5, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Photoshop and Substance 3D Painter.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. PT Autotube Crew Studio works with clients worldwide through online communication and secure digital project delivery.

Can you create custom CGI and animation?

Yes. Every project is developed according to the client's objectives, branding and production requirements.

Can you work with an existing creative team?

Yes. We can collaborate with existing creative teams, agencies, production companies and independent professionals depending on the requirements of the project.

Can you handle only one stage of a project?

Yes. Our studio can support individual production stages such as 3D modeling, texturing, environments, animation, rendering, compositing, motion graphics or post-production.

Do you accept international projects?

Yes. Our digital production workflow allows us to communicate and collaborate remotely with clients and creative partners in different locations.

How can I request a quotation?

Simply visit our Contact page or send us your project details, references, required deliverables and production requirements. Our team can review the information and discuss the appropriate production approach.
