Studio and services

What does PT Autotube Crew Studio do

PT Autotube Crew Studio focuses on CGI 3D animation visual effects and video production The right service depends on the goals and requirements of your project

What types of projects can I ask about

You can contact us about product visualization CGI animation visual effects motion graphics video production and other visual content needs

Can you work with an existing creative team

Project collaboration can be discussed with your agency production company brand or creative team The roles and deliverables should be agreed before work begins

Can I enquire from outside Indonesia

Yes You can contact the studio from another country to discuss a project Communication schedule payment terms and delivery details should be confirmed for each project

Quotes and planning

How do I request a quote

Use the Request a Quote page and share your project goals expected deliverables and timeline Add reference materials or a budget range if available

How much does a project cost

Costs depend on the project scope complexity production schedule and deliverables The studio can discuss a quote after reviewing the project requirements

How long will production take

Timing depends on the project scope and production requirements A proposed schedule should be agreed before production starts

Are revisions included

Revision rounds and approval stages depend on the project agreement Confirm them in writing before work begins

Files and project rights

What formats can you deliver

Delivery formats depend on how you plan to use the final work Agree on file types sizes aspect ratios and delivery method as part of the project scope

Who owns the final work

Ownership usage and licensing terms should be stated in the written project agreement Review those terms before approving production

Can you keep a project confidential

Discuss confidentiality needs before sharing sensitive materials Any confidentiality terms should be agreed in writing

Will you show my project in your portfolio

Portfolio use should be discussed and agreed with the client Confidential or unreleased work should not be shown without permission
