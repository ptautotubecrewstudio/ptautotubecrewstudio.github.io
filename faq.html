General

What does PT Autotube Crew Studio do?
PT Autotube Crew Studio is a visual production studio focused on CGI, 3D animation, VFX, video production and cinematic visual content.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. Projects can be discussed and managed remotely with clients, agencies, brands and production partners in different markets.
What type of projects do you accept?
We work on a range of visual projects including brand films, product visualization, CGI campaigns, 3D animation, VFX, promotional content and cinematic productions.

Services

What is the difference between CGI and 3D animation?
CGI refers broadly to computer-generated imagery, while 3D animation focuses on creating movement and storytelling using 3D assets, environments, cameras and animation. A project can use both CGI and 3D animation.
Do you provide VFX?
Yes. VFX can include digital compositing, visual effects integration, cleanup, environment work and other visual-effects requirements depending on the project.
Can you produce a complete video from concept to final delivery?
Yes. Depending on the project scope, production can include creative development, visual direction, 3D or VFX production, animation, editing, rendering and final delivery.
Can you create product visualization?
Yes. Product visualization can include detailed 3D models, materials, lighting, animation, product demonstrations and cinematic presentation.

Production Process

What happens after I contact the studio?
We first review your project requirements, objectives, references and expected deliverables. From there, we can discuss scope, production approach, schedule and the next stage of the project.
Do I need a finished script before contacting you?
No. You can contact us even when your project is still at the concept stage. We can discuss the creative direction and determine what needs to be developed before production.
Can you work from an existing storyboard or concept?
Yes. Existing scripts, storyboards, concept art, references and production materials can be incorporated into the workflow where appropriate.
How long does a project take?
Production time depends on scope, complexity, duration, number of assets, animation requirements, VFX complexity and revision requirements. A realistic schedule is determined after reviewing the project brief.

Pricing

How is the project price calculated?
Pricing depends on factors such as creative scope, production complexity, number of shots, asset requirements, animation length, VFX requirements, rendering and delivery specifications.
Do you have fixed prices for your services?
Many production projects require a custom quotation because every brief is different. The most accurate approach is to review the requirements before providing an estimate.
Can I request a quotation before committing to production?
Yes. You can submit your project requirements through our Request a Quote page so the scope can be reviewed before production begins.

Revisions & Delivery

Are revisions included?
Revision arrangements depend on the agreed production scope. The quotation or project agreement should define the applicable review and revision stages.
What file formats can you deliver?
Final delivery formats depend on the intended platform and project requirements. Common deliverables can include high-quality video files, image sequences and platform-specific versions.
Can you create different versions for social media?
Yes. Multiple aspect ratios, resolutions and versions can be planned when required, including formats suitable for different digital platforms.

Confidentiality

Can you work under an NDA?
NDA and confidentiality requirements can be discussed as part of the project onboarding process and should be agreed before sensitive materials are shared.
Can confidential project materials be provided before the quotation?
If confidential information is required for project evaluation, the appropriate confidentiality arrangements can be discussed before sensitive materials are exchanged.

Starting a Project

What should I provide when requesting a quote?
Helpful information includes the project objective, creative concept, approximate duration, required services, target audience, deadline, references, expected deliverables and estimated budget when available.
What if I do not know my exact budget?
That is fine. You can indicate that you are unsure of the budget and provide as much information about the project scope as possible. The production requirements are important for determining an appropriate approach.
How do I start?
The easiest way to begin is to submit your project through the Request a Quote page. You can also contact the studio directly if you would prefer to discuss the project first.
