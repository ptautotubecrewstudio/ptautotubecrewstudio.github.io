OUR PROJECT LIFECYCLE

SEVEN STAGES. ONE CLEAR PROCESS.

A practical project framework that can be adapted to the requirements, complexity and deliverables of each assignment.

01

Discovery & Brief

Understanding the client's idea, business objectives, audience, requirements and desired outcomes. Establishing the initial project direction and key questions.

02

Scope & Proposal

Defining the project scope, proposed deliverables, responsibilities, resource requirements, estimated timeline and commercial terms.

03

Planning & Pre-Production

Developing the project plan, creative direction, technical approach, production schedule and required materials before execution.

04

Production & Development

Executing the agreed creative or technical work. This may include animation, rendering, filming, visual effects or platform development.

05

Review & Refinement

Handover & Next Steps

Providing the agreed final materials, documentation and handover information, followed by any agreed support arrangements.

PROJECT MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

KEEPING PROJECTS ON TRACK.

Project scope, deliverables, approval requirements, responsibilities and commercial terms should be documented before execution begins.

Where a request changes the approved scope, additional work, resources, costs or timelines may need to be reviewed and agreed before implementation.

Feedback, revisions, project milestones and final handover are managed according to the individual project agreement.

This approach helps both parties maintain clarity about expectations, responsibilities and project boundaries.

PROCESS INFORMATION

WORKFLOW QUESTIONS.

Does every project follow the same workflow?

The main project framework provides consistency, but the actual stages depend on the project's scope, technical needs, production type and agreed deliverables.

Can clients participate in production decisions?

Yes. Clients participate through agreed review points, creative discussions, feedback and milestone approvals. The exact approval process is defined for each project.

How are project revisions managed?

Revision requirements, review rounds and approval stages are established in the project scope or applicable agreement. Requests outside the agreed scope may require additional discussion.

How is the production timeline determined?

Timelines are evaluated according to the project scope, complexity, available resources, production dependencies, approval schedules and agreed deliverables.

Will clients receive project source files?

Source files, working project files and additional materials are provided only where specifically included in the applicable project agreement.
