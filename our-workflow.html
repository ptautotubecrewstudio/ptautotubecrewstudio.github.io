PROJECT LIFECYCLE

A Structured Project From Brief to Handover.

Seven stages provide a framework for project planning, execution, review and delivery. Their application depends on the assignment, production requirements and approved scope.

Project Discovery

Establish the project objectives, intended audience, creative direction, technical requirements and expected outcome. Review available references and identify outstanding requirements.

Output: Project Brief

Scope and Proposal

Define the required work, deliverables, responsibilities, dependencies, assumptions, review arrangements, schedule and commercial considerations.

Output: Defined Project Scope

Planning and Pre-Production

Establish the production schedule, creative approach, required resources and technical preparation. Develop the platform architecture or production plan where applicable.

Output: Approved Production Plan

Production and Development

Execute the approved creative, production or development activities. Coordinate the work against the project schedule, requirements and agreed milestones.

Output: Work in Progress

Review and Refinement

Present project work at scheduled review points, consolidate feedback and complete revisions according to the approved brief and agreed change arrangements.

Output: Reviewed Project Work

Quality Control and Delivery

Review the completed work against the agreed creative, technical and delivery specifications. Prepare the approved outputs in the required formats.

Output: Final Deliverables

Handover and Support

Transfer the agreed deliverables and supporting documentation. Confirm completion requirements and discuss any additional support or follow-up arrangements.

Output: Project Handover

The lifecycle is adapted to each assignment. Not every project requires identical stages, review arrangements or timelines. The applicable workflow is established during project planning and scope definition.

QUALITY CONTROL

Quality Checks Throughout Production.

Quality reviews are based on the approved brief, technical specifications and acceptance requirements. Checks take place at relevant production milestones and before final handover.

Creative Review

Assess visual direction, storytelling, design, composition and creative consistency against the approved brief and reference materials.

Technical Review

Check functionality, compatibility, rendering, file integrity, output specifications and other technical requirements relevant to the assignment.

Scope and Deliverables

Verify that the completed work corresponds to the agreed scope, specified outputs, file formats and documented project requirements.

Final Approval

Resolve outstanding review points, confirm the approved outputs and prepare the final deliverables for handover.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Clear Responsibilities. Coordinated Project Delivery.

Project execution depends on timely communication, clear decisions and defined responsibilities between the studio and the client.

Studio Responsibilities

Review the brief, clarify requirements, prepare the project plan, coordinate production or development activities, communicate progress and prepare the deliverables included in the agreement.

Client Responsibilities

Provide accurate project information and required materials, identify authorised decision-makers, review submissions, consolidate feedback and provide approvals within the agreed schedule.

Project scope, responsibilities, approval requirements, schedules and commercial terms should be documented before execution begins. Changes to the original requirements may affect resources, costs and delivery dates. Revised work should be agreed before implementation.

PROJECT INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Workflow Questions.

Information about project planning, client reviews, revisions and final delivery.

Does every project follow the same workflow?

The overall framework remains consistent, but individual stages depend on the project type, scope, complexity and technical requirements. Digital platform development and animated production, for example, involve different specialist activities.

Can clients provide feedback during production?

Yes. Client reviews and approvals take place at agreed milestones. The number of review rounds, feedback format and approval responsibilities are defined during project planning.

How is the project timeline determined?

The schedule is based on the required deliverables, project complexity, available resources, production dependencies, review cycles and approval requirements. The proposed timeline is confirmed as part of the project agreement.

What happens when project requirements change?

Requested changes are reviewed to assess their impact on the scope, resources, schedule and cost. Additional work or revised deliverables must be agreed before the updated requirements are implemented.

Are source files and working project files included?

Source files, editable project files and additional working materials are provided only where they are specifically included in the relevant project agreement. Final delivery formats should be established before production begins.

How can I start a project discussion?

Use the Contact page to share your project concept, objectives, expected deliverables and initial references. The studio can review the information and discuss the appropriate next steps.
