CLARITY AT EVERY STAGE

A considered process. One shared direction.

Every project begins with an idea, a business objective or a creative challenge. Before production or development begins, the team needs a shared understanding of what the work must achieve, who it is for and what the final deliverables need to include.

Our project framework establishes that foundation, then adapts to the requirements of each assignment. Digital platform development and creative production follow different technical paths, while sharing the same emphasis on planning, communication, review and delivery.

THE PROJECT LIFECYCLE

Seven stages. One clear process.

A practical framework that can be adjusted to the scale, complexity, production type and agreed scope of each project.

Discovery & Brief

Understand the objectives, audience, creative direction, technical needs and intended outcome. Identify the questions that need to be resolved before planning.

Output: Shared project understanding

Scope & Proposal

Define the work, deliverables, responsibilities, dependencies, assumptions, review points and commercial considerations for the proposed assignment.

Output: Agreed project scope

Planning & Pre-Production

Develop the schedule, creative approach, resources, production plan, platform architecture or other preparation needed for execution.

Output: Approved plan and direction

Production & Development

Carry out the agreed creative, production or development work, with progress coordinated against the approved direction and milestones.

Output: Work in progress

Review & Refinement

Present work at agreed review points, collect consolidated feedback and make refinements in line with the approved brief and change process.

Output: Reviewed project work

Quality Control & Delivery

Check the work against agreed creative, technical and delivery requirements, then prepare the approved outputs in the required formats.

Output: Approved deliverables

Handover & Next Steps

Provide the agreed handover materials, document any relevant completion details and identify follow-up support or future opportunities where applicable.

Output: Project handover

The lifecycle is a flexible project framework, not a fixed promise that every assignment uses identical stages or timelines. The applicable process is confirmed as part of the project scope.

WORKING TOGETHER

A successful project requires shared responsibility.

Clear communication helps teams make decisions, manage review points and keep work aligned with the intended outcome.

Our Responsibilities

Understand the brief, clarify requirements, plan the agreed work, coordinate the relevant production or development activities, communicate progress and prepare the agreed deliverables.

Client Responsibilities

Provide the required project information and materials, identify decision-makers, review submissions at agreed points, consolidate feedback and confirm approvals or changes in a timely manner.

Project scope, responsibilities, approval requirements, timelines and commercial terms should be documented before execution begins. A request that changes the agreed scope may require a review of resources, costs and delivery dates before the additional work proceeds.

PROCESS INFORMATION

Workflow questions.

Some practical points about project planning, feedback and delivery.

Does every project follow the same workflow?

The overall framework is consistent, but the actual stages depend on project type, scope, complexity, technical needs and agreed deliverables. A digital platform and an animated production, for example, require different specialist steps.

Can clients provide feedback during production?

Yes. Client reviews and approvals are planned at agreed milestones. The number and timing of review rounds, feedback format and approval responsibilities should be defined in the project scope.

How is the project timeline determined?

Timing is evaluated against the deliverables, project complexity, production dependencies, required resources, review cycles and approval schedule. A project timeline should be confirmed as part of the agreed plan.

What happens when the project requirements change?

Changes are reviewed to understand their impact on scope, resources, schedule and cost. Any additional work or revised deliverables should be agreed before the changed scope is implemented.

Are source files and working project files included?

Source files, editable project files and additional working materials are provided only where they are specifically included in the applicable project agreement. Final delivery formats should be agreed in advance.

How do I start a project discussion?

Use the Contact page to share your project concept, objectives, expected deliverables and any initial references. The studio can then review the information and discuss an appropriate next step.
