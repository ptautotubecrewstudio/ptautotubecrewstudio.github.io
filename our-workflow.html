Our Production Workflow

A structured and collaborative production process designed to move projects from initial ideas to polished final delivery with clarity, quality and efficiency.

From Concept to Final Delivery

Our workflow combines creative development, technical production, review and quality control to keep every project organized and focused.

STEP 01

Project Brief

We begin by understanding the client's objectives, creative direction, audience, deliverables, schedule and technical requirements.

STEP 02

Concept & Planning

The project is developed through references, visual direction, storyboards, planning and production requirements.

STEP 03

Pre-Production

We prepare assets, scenes, production schedules, technical setups and other elements required before production begins.

STEP 04

3D Production

Our artists create models, environments, materials, lighting and other 3D assets according to the approved creative direction.

STEP 05

Animation & VFX

Animation, simulations, effects, camera work and motion design are developed and integrated into the production.

STEP 06

Lighting & Rendering

Scenes are refined with lighting, materials and rendering processes to achieve the required visual quality.

STEP 07

Compositing & Post Production

Rendered elements are composited and enhanced with color, effects, editing and other post-production techniques.

STEP 08

Review & Quality Control

We review the work against the project requirements and address necessary revisions before final approval.

STEP 09

Final Delivery

Approved content is prepared in the required formats and delivered according to the agreed project specifications.

How We Work

Our production approach is built around clear communication, organized processes and consistent quality.

Clear Communication

We maintain communication throughout production so project objectives, feedback and updates remain clear.

Organized Production

Each project follows a structured workflow designed to manage assets, tasks, reviews and delivery efficiently.

Regular Reviews

Review points allow creative and technical decisions to be evaluated before the project moves to the next stage.

Flexible Collaboration

We can collaborate remotely with clients, agencies, production companies and distributed creative teams.

Quality Focus

Quality checks are integrated into the production process to maintain consistent visual and technical standards.

Reliable Delivery

Final assets are prepared carefully according to agreed specifications, formats and delivery requirements.
