Our Production Workflow

A clear and organized workflow helps us transform creative ideas into high-quality CGI, 3D animation, visual effects and digital content.

Our Creative Process

From the first project discussion to final delivery, each stage is organized to maintain creative quality, technical accuracy and efficient communication.

01

Project Brief & Requirements

We begin by understanding the project objectives, creative vision, target audience, deliverables, technical requirements and expected timeline.

02

Creative Direction & Planning

We establish the visual direction, references, production approach, scope and key creative requirements before production begins.

03

Pre-Production

Storyboards, asset planning, scene preparation, schedules and other production elements are organized to create a strong foundation.

04

3D Asset Production

Our team develops models, environments, materials, textures and other 3D assets required for the project.

05

Animation & Visual Effects

Animation, simulations, camera movement, visual effects and motion elements are produced according to the approved direction.

06

Lighting & Rendering

Scenes are refined through lighting, materials, rendering and technical optimization to achieve the required visual quality.

07

Compositing & Post Production

Rendered elements are combined and refined through compositing, editing, color work and additional post-production processes.

08

Review & Revisions

The work is reviewed against the project requirements. Client feedback and necessary revisions are incorporated before final approval.

09

Quality Control & Final Delivery

Final assets are checked and prepared in the required formats before being delivered according to the agreed project specifications.

Production Stages

Our workflow can support different types of creative production while maintaining a structured process.

CGI & 3D Production

3D modeling, environments, materials, texturing, lighting and photorealistic rendering.

Animation

Character, product, camera and cinematic animation for films, commercials and digital content.

Visual Effects

VFX elements, simulations, compositing and digital effects for commercial and cinematic productions.

Motion Graphics

Animated graphics, promotional visuals, titles and digital content for brands and media platforms.

Product Visualization

Detailed product models, materials, lighting and marketing renders designed for professional presentation.

Post Production

Editing, compositing, color refinement and final content preparation for different delivery platforms.

Built for Creative Collaboration

Our workflow allows us to collaborate with businesses, agencies, production companies and distributed creative teams while keeping communication and project progress organized.
