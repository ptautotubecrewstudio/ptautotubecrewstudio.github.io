VIDEO PRODUCTION

From the Production Brief To the Final Film.

PT Autotube Crew Studio develops video and advertising productions for commercial communication, brand promotion, corporate presentations and digital distribution.

Each assignment begins with an assessment of the brief, target audience, communication objectives, creative direction, production format and delivery requirements. These details establish the production scope and the appropriate execution plan.

Our pre-to-post production approach connects creative planning, production execution, editorial work, visual finishing and final delivery within the agreed project requirements.
