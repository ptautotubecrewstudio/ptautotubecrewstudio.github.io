Our Process

A Structured Production Workflow

Each stage is scoped for the project so creative, technical and delivery requirements stay aligned.

01 / DISCOVER

Brief & Direction

Discuss objectives, audience, message, references and production requirements.

02 / PREPARE

Pre-Production

Plan the creative approach, schedule, shot list and resources for the project.

03 / PRODUCE

Production

Film and create the visual material according to the approved production plan.

04 / FINISH

Post-Production

Edit, refine and prepare final content in the agreed formats and specifications.
