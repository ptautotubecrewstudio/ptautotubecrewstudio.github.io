A COLLABORATIVE STUDIO

The right team for the right vision.

Strong production partnerships begin with shared expectations. We aim to understand the brief, creative direction, audience, technical requirements and intended delivery before defining a suitable production approach.

Our capabilities can support a project as a focused production assignment or as part of a wider pipeline involving a client team, agency, production company or specialist studio. The responsibilities and scope are agreed for each engagement.

Based in Jakarta, Indonesia, AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO is positioned to discuss projects and production collaboration with teams in Indonesia and internationally.

COLLABORATION QUESTIONS

A few useful things to know.

Do you work with international production teams?

We are based in Jakarta, Indonesia and welcome enquiries about suitable projects and production collaboration from teams in Indonesia and other markets. Scope, communication and delivery arrangements are discussed for each project.

Can you support only one stage of a project?

Project discussions can cover a complete production assignment or a defined specialist requirement. The available scope and responsibilities are confirmed during project assessment.

How do we start a collaboration?

Share your project concept, objectives, reference material, expected deliverables and target schedule through the Contact page. The studio can then review the information and discuss the next steps.

Are the sectors on this page existing clients?

No. These are potential client and partner categories based on the studio's capabilities. They should not be interpreted as confirmed client relationships, signed partnerships or completed engagements.
