About AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO

PT AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO is an Indonesia-based company operating across digital platforms and creative production. Its creative production focus includes 3D animation, computer-generated imagery (CGI), visual effects (VFX), film and video production, and digital creative content. 05Brand & media guidance

Accurate information. Responsible use.

Media materials should be handled carefully and used only for the purposes and permissions confirmed by the company.

Use approved brand assets

Request the current official logo and brand materials. Do not redraw, recolour, distort or modify the company logo without written approval.

Verify facts before publication

Confirm company descriptions, names, titles, project credits, dates and other factual statements with the studio before publication.

Respect project permissions

Portfolio images, videos, production stills and other creative assets may be subject to copyright, licensing, client approval or partner restrictions.

Protect confidential information

Unreleased projects, unpublished artwork, private business discussions and confidential commercial information are not public media assets.

07Common questions

Press & media questions.

Can I use the company logo from this website?

Please contact the studio to request the appropriate approved logo file and confirm the permitted use. Do not assume that a displayed image grants unrestricted reproduction rights.

Can I request an interview or company statement?

Yes. Send your publication details, subject, proposed questions or topic, preferred interview format and deadline through the Contact page. Requests are reviewed individually.

Are all portfolio images available for editorial use?

No. Published portfolio material may have copyright, licensing, client or partner restrictions. Request permission for each proposed use before reproduction or publication.

Can I receive a company profile or press document?

Submit a request describing the document or information required and its intended use. Availability and approval will be confirmed by the studio.

How do I submit a media enquiry?

Use the official Contact page and include your name, organization, enquiry subject, requested materials and publication deadline.
