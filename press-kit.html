ABOUT AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO.

PT AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO is an Indonesia-based company operating across digital platforms and creative production. Its creative production focus includes 3D animation, computer-generated imagery (CGI), visual effects (VFX), film and video production and digital creative content. The company brings together creativity, technology and visual storytelling to develop digital and creative experiences. AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO also explores business, production and technology collaborations with relevant organizations and professionals.

BRAND & MEDIA GUIDANCE

RESPONSIBLE BRAND REPRESENTATION.

Professional media communication should respect company identity and the usage permissions of supplied materials.

Approved Brand Assets

Please request official brand materials directly from the company before publishing or reproducing them.

  • Use the supplied approved logo files.
  • Preserve the original logo proportions.
  • Avoid unauthorized color or design changes.
  • Do not imply an official endorsement without written approval.

Media & Publication Guidelines

Media representatives should verify information and obtain permission for materials that are not publicly approved.

  • Verify company descriptions and facts.
  • Request permission before using unpublished production materials.
  • Confirm image and content usage rights.
  • Do not publish confidential project information without approval.
