PARTNERSHIP INFORMATION

COMMON QUESTIONS.

What types of partnerships does the company consider?

We welcome enquiries involving production collaboration, co-production, outsourcing, digital technology, infrastructure, creative talent, commercial relationships and strategic business development. Every proposal is evaluated according to its suitability.

Does AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO work with international partners?

We welcome enquiries from international businesses, studios, technology providers and creative professionals. Potential collaborations are considered subject to operational, commercial and applicable legal requirements.

Can production studios collaborate on animation and film projects?

Yes. Suitable animation, CGI, VFX and film production collaborations can be discussed on a project-specific basis. Responsibilities, production scope and deliverables should be agreed in writing.

Can technology companies provide render farm or infrastructure services?

We welcome technical enquiries regarding rendering infrastructure, computing resources, storage, software and production hardware. Technical specifications, capacity, security and commercial arrangements would need to be evaluated.

How can an organization become a partner?

Organizations can contact our company with a brief introduction, capabilities, relevant portfolio and proposed collaboration objectives. Our team can then review the enquiry and discuss suitable next steps.

Does submitting a partnership proposal guarantee acceptance?

No. All partnership proposals are subject to individual review, business suitability, due diligence where appropriate and mutually agreed terms.
