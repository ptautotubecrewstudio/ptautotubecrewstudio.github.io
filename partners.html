Professional Project Governance

Clear Agreements. Transparent Responsibilities.

Successful production partnerships require more than creative alignment. Every project should have a clearly documented agreement covering responsibilities, approvals, revisions, ownership and project scope.

01

Project Scope & Deliverables

Document the agreed project scope, production requirements, deliverables, formats, milestones and responsibilities before work begins.

02

Client Approval Process

Identify authorized approvers, milestone approval requirements, feedback procedures and written approval records.

03

Revision Management

Define the included revision rounds, feedback consolidation process and procedures for additional revisions.

04

Source Files & Ownership

Specify ownership, usage rights, editable source files, project assets, third-party licences and agreed file handover conditions.

05

Scope Change Control

Manage new requirements, additional deliverables and changes to creative direction through documented change requests and agreed adjustments.

06

Commercial & Delivery Terms

Establish project fees, payment milestones, deadlines, delivery conditions, confidentiality and applicable contractual responsibilities.

Formal Project Agreement Framework

The following controls should be documented in the applicable project agreement before production begins:

  • Written confirmation of the agreed project brief and scope.
  • Named client and studio representatives responsible for milestone approvals.
  • Agreed revision limits and procedures for additional revision requests.
  • Defined ownership and transfer conditions for deliverables and editable source files.
  • Written change requests covering new requirements, additional costs and schedule adjustments.
  • Defined payment terms, delivery milestones and confidentiality obligations.

Specific contractual rights and obligations must be established in the signed agreement for each project. This framework is a planning structure and does not replace legal review or a formal contract.
