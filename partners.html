CLEAR AGREEMENTS. DEFINED RESPONSIBILITIES.

A shared understanding before production begins.

Every collaboration should be supported by written project terms appropriate to its scope. The final agreement governs the project; the points below describe topics that should be clarified, not substitute contract terms.

01 / SCOPE

Project scope & deliverables

Document the work, formats, milestones, responsibilities, assumptions and exclusions agreed for the project.

02 / APPROVALS

Review & approvals

Identify authorized reviewers, approval stages, feedback requirements and written sign-off procedures.

03 / REVISIONS

Revision management

Define included revision rounds, feedback consolidation and how additional revision requests are evaluated.

04 / OWNERSHIP

Source files & intellectual property

Clarify ownership, usage rights, licences, third-party materials and any source-file handover obligations in the applicable agreement.

05 / CHANGE CONTROL

Scope and schedule changes

Agree how changes to requirements, resources, costs or timelines are reviewed and approved before extra work proceeds.

06 / COMMERCIAL

Commercial & delivery terms

Record fees, payment milestones, delivery dates, confidentiality requirements and other relevant commercial conditions.
