ABOUT THIS RESOURCE CENTRE

Clear information. Consistent representation.

PT AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO is an Indonesia-based company focused on digital platforms and creative production. The studio's creative capabilities include 3D animation, CGI, VFX, cinematic and video production, and digital content creation.

This page brings together the currently available public brand resource and links to company information already published on the studio website. Additional materials can be requested for review and approval.

VISUAL IDENTITY REFERENCE

A consistent visual language

The website's core visual palette is built around dark neutrals, crimson red and white. These swatches are a quick website reference, not a replacement for a formal brand-guidelines document.
