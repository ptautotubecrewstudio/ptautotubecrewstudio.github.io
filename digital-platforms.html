TECHNICAL AND CREATIVE REQUIREMENTS

Technology Defined By the Project Requirements.

Platform architecture and technology decisions should reflect the purpose of the website, its expected users, required functionality and future operational needs. Design, navigation and content structure should support the same defined objectives.

Back-end systems, databases, authentication, payment services, third-party integrations, hosting and ongoing maintenance are considered where required. These components are subject to technical assessment and must be included in the agreed project scope.

The development approach also takes account of responsive behaviour, accessibility, performance, search engine requirements and the technical environment in which the platform will operate.
