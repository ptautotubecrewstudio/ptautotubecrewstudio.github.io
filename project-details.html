DEFINING THE WORK

CLEAR SCOPE. CLEAR EXPECTATIONS.

A documented scope helps both parties understand which activities are included and what is outside the approved project.

01. Included in Project Scope

  • Approved project objectives and creative brief.
  • Agreed production or development activities.
  • Specified work stages and milestones.
  • Defined review and approval procedures.
  • Agreed final deliverables and output formats.
  • Documented commercial and delivery conditions.

02. Items Requiring Separate Approval

  • Additional features outside the approved brief.
  • Major creative direction changes.
  • Additional deliverables or formats.
  • Extra revisions beyond the agreed allowance.
  • Additional equipment, software or production resources.
  • Changes that affect the budget or delivery timeline.

PROFESSIONAL COLLABORATION

SHARED RESPONSIBILITIES.

Clear responsibilities help maintain communication, project accountability and delivery expectations.

Studio Responsibilities

  • Review project requirements and objectives.
  • Establish agreed production scope and workflow.
  • Coordinate relevant production activities.
  • Communicate project progress and relevant developments.
  • Perform agreed quality checks and reviews.
  • Prepare and deliver the agreed outputs.

Client Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate project information and requirements.
  • Supply authorized references, content and assets.
  • Nominate appropriate project contacts and decision-makers.
  • Review submitted work and provide clear feedback.
  • Provide approvals within agreed review periods.
  • Meet agreed commercial and contractual obligations.

FORMAL PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

DOCUMENTATION PROTECTS BOTH PARTIES.

Before a project starts, relevant commercial and operational conditions should be properly documented.

Depending on the nature of the engagement, documentation may include a project proposal, statement of work, commercial quotation, production schedule, milestone approvals and a formal agreement.

Payment arrangements, revision limits, intellectual property, confidentiality, source-file ownership, licensing and project termination conditions should be clearly established.

Any changes to approved project requirements should follow an appropriate change-control process and receive the necessary approval.

PROJECT QUESTIONS.

What information should a project brief contain?

A useful project brief includes objectives, background, target audience, creative references, required services, intended deliverables, timeline and available budget information.

Can project requirements change after production begins?

Changes may be discussed during production. However, any change that affects approved scope, cost, resources or schedule should be assessed and agreed before implementation.

How are final project deliverables determined?

Final deliverables are defined in the project proposal or formal agreement. File formats, resolution, versions and handover requirements should be specified in advance.

Will clients own the source files?

Ownership, licensing and handover of source files must be expressly addressed in the relevant project agreement. Delivery of final outputs does not automatically mean that all underlying working files are transferred.

Can international projects be discussed?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from local and international businesses, production studios and creative collaborators. Relevant commercial and operational arrangements can be discussed individually.

How can I submit a project enquiry?

Visit our Contact page and share your project description, requirements and expected deliverables. Our team can then discuss the appropriate next steps.
