3D Animation and CGI

Statue Launch Reveal Animation

A cinematic visual experience designed to transform a statue reveal into a memorable moment through 3D animation lighting CGI and storytelling.

PROJECT VIDEO OR MAIN PROJECT IMAGE WILL BE ADDED HERE

The Challenge

The objective was to create a visually powerful reveal that could generate anticipation and present the statue in a cinematic and engaging way.

The project required a combination of creative direction 3D animation lighting and visual effects to transform the concept into a polished final presentation.

Our Solution

PT Autotube Crew Studio developed a complete visual approach beginning with concept development and visual planning followed by 3D production animation lighting and final rendering.

Cinematic camera movement dramatic lighting and carefully designed animation were used to create a strong sense of scale anticipation and impact.

Production Process

The production process included creative planning 3D asset preparation animation scene development lighting rendering and final post production.

Every stage was developed to maintain visual consistency and deliver a final result suitable for presentations campaigns social media or event displays.

Final Outcome

The completed animation delivered a cinematic reveal designed to capture attention and present the subject with a premium visual identity.
