01Understanding Your Project

Every detail matters before production begins.

Before a creative or digital project starts, the people involved need a shared understanding of its objectives, audience, scope and expected outcomes. These details help establish the right production approach and make responsibilities clearer.

AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO considers the creative vision alongside technical requirements, production resources, schedule, approvals and agreed deliverables. The level of detail will vary according to the nature and scale of each project.

02Project Foundation

Six elements that define the brief.

A useful project brief records the information needed to evaluate, plan and agree the work before execution.

01 / IDENTIFICATION

Project identification

Project title, organization, main contacts, project category and the people responsible for approvals.

02 / OBJECTIVES

Project objectives

The creative purpose, intended audience, key message, problem to solve and expected outcome.

03 / SCOPE

Scope of work

The agreed activities, required services, production stages, assumptions and any exclusions.

04 / RESOURCES

Resources & technology

Reference materials, supplied assets, specialist capabilities, technical needs and production dependencies.

05 / TIMELINE

Timeline & milestones

Target dates, review windows, approval points, delivery sequence and dependencies that affect the schedule.

06 / OUTPUTS

Final deliverables

Agreed outputs, formats, resolutions, versions, usage requirements and handover expectations.

04Defining the Work

Clear scope.
Clear expectations.

A written scope helps both parties understand which activities and outputs are included in the approved project and which require separate discussion.

01. Included in the agreed scope

  • Services and production activities stated in the proposal or statement of work.
  • Deliverables, formats and versions specifically listed in the agreement.
  • Milestones, review points and approval responsibilities agreed for the project.
  • Revision rounds and feedback process defined in the commercial terms.
  • Specified handover items and delivery conditions.

02. Items to clarify separately

  • New creative directions or requirements beyond the approved brief.
  • Additional versions, formats, scenes, shots or features not included in scope.
  • Extra revision rounds or substantial changes after approval milestones.
  • Third-party licences, supplied materials or external costs where applicable.
  • Source files, editable project assets or additional usage rights unless agreed.

The final scope is not determined by this overview page. The applicable proposal, quotation and signed project agreement define the actual responsibilities, inclusions, exclusions and commercial terms for each engagement.

05Completed Project Outputs

What will be delivered?

Deliverables should be listed clearly before production. Final files and handover materials depend on the agreed service, scope and project requirements.

Video deliverables

Approved final videos, specified resolutions, aspect ratios, file formats and agreed versions.

Animation outputs

Approved animation sequences, renders, frame rates, scene outputs and final video exports.

Visual assets

Agreed CGI stills, rendered imagery, visual effects shots, graphics or other specified assets.

Digital platforms

Agreed website or platform functions, pages, responsive behavior, documentation and deployment items.

Project documentation

Specified project notes, approved references, delivery records or handover documentation.

Working files

Editable project files, source materials and production assets only where included in the agreement.

06Project Management

Track progress through defined milestones.

Milestones help structure communication, review and approvals. Their number and sequence are adapted to the project.

STAGE 01

Project approval

Confirm the brief, scope, quotation, responsibilities and commercial terms.

STAGE 02

Planning approval

Review concepts, scripts, storyboards, technical plans or platform designs as required.

STAGE 03

Production review

Review agreed work-in-progress outputs and provide consolidated feedback at planned points.

STAGE 04

Final approval

Confirm the agreed final outputs and complete the documented handover process.

07Professional Collaboration

Shared responsibilities.
Better coordination.

Clear responsibilities help maintain accountability and keep project communication aligned from briefing through delivery.

Studio responsibilities

  • Review the brief and clarify production requirements.
  • Plan the agreed work and coordinate assigned production activities.
  • Communicate progress, dependencies and review requirements.
  • Prepare and deliver the outputs defined in the agreement.

Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate briefs, references and required materials.
  • Identify authorized decision-makers and provide consolidated feedback.
  • Review work at agreed milestones and provide timely approvals.
  • Meet the agreed commercial, access and project coordination obligations.

08Formal Project Documentation

Good documentation protects both parties.

Depending on the engagement, project records may include a project brief, proposal, statement of work, commercial quotation, production schedule, milestone approvals and a formal agreement.

Payment arrangements, revision limits, intellectual property, confidentiality, licensing, source-file ownership, usage rights and project changes should be clearly established in the applicable documents before work begins.

09Project Information

Project questions.

Some common points to consider before starting a creative production or digital platform project.

What should a useful project brief include?

A useful brief explains the objectives, background, intended audience, creative references, required services, expected deliverables, timeline and any available budget information.

Can project requirements change after work begins?

Changes can be discussed. Any request that affects the approved scope, cost, resources or schedule should be reviewed and agreed before implementation.

Are source files included in every project?

Not automatically. Working files, editable project assets and additional materials are supplied only where specifically included in the applicable proposal or agreement.

How are revisions and approvals managed?

Revision rounds, feedback procedures and approval milestones are defined in the project scope or agreement. This helps establish a shared review process.
