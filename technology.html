Technology Behind Our Creative Production

PT Autotube Crew Studio uses professional digital production tools and modern workflows to create high-quality CGI, 3D animation, visual effects, visualization and digital content for clients worldwide.

Our Production Technology

Our technology choices support different stages of creative production, from 3D development and real-time environments to compositing, editing and final delivery.

01

Blender

Blender supports our 3D production activities including modeling, sculpting, texturing, animation, lighting, rendering and scene development.

  • 3D Modeling
  • Environment Creation
  • Animation
  • Lighting and Rendering
02

Unreal Engine 5

Unreal Engine 5 provides real-time capabilities for interactive environments, visualization, cinematic scenes and modern digital experiences.

  • Real-Time Visualization
  • Virtual Environments
  • Cinematic Scenes
  • Interactive Experiences
03

Adobe Creative Cloud

Adobe Creative Cloud supports graphic design, compositing, motion design, image preparation and other creative production requirements.

  • Graphic Design
  • Motion Design
  • Compositing
  • Creative Asset Preparation
04

DaVinci Resolve

DaVinci Resolve supports video editing, color workflows and finishing requirements for professional digital productions.

  • Video Editing
  • Color Correction
  • Color Grading
  • Final Production

Technology Across Our Workflow

Different production stages require different tools. Our workflow combines creative development with technical production and final finishing.

01

Development

Concept development, planning and preparation of digital production assets.

02

3D Production

Modeling, environments, animation, materials, lighting and scene development.

03

Real-Time & Effects

Real-time visualization, cinematic scenes and digital effects production.

04

Finishing

Editing, color work, compositing and preparation of final deliverables.

Technology That Supports Creativity

Our production tools are selected according to project requirements, creative goals and delivery needs. We focus on using technology as a practical part of the creative process.
