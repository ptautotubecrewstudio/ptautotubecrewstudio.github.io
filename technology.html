CONNECTED PRODUCTION PIPELINE

From creative development to final delivery.

The pipeline is adapted to the scope and format of each assignment. A project may use selected stages or a more integrated pre-to-post production process.

STAGE 01

Development

Understand the brief, define creative direction, assess technical requirements and prepare the production approach.

STAGE 02

Asset creation

Prepare the digital assets, scenes, environments, project elements and production materials required.

STAGE 03

Production

Execute the agreed animation, CGI, VFX, video or digital development work using suitable technical methods.

STAGE 04

Post-production

Refine, composite, edit, review and finish project content according to the approved direction.

QUALITY & TECHNICAL REVIEW

Creative quality.
Technical confidence.

Quality review is considered throughout the production process, not only at the final export stage. Visual consistency, creative direction, technical compatibility and agreed deliverables are reviewed according to the project requirements.

Final formats, source files, project documentation and handover materials depend on the approved scope and applicable project agreement. Technical specifications are confirmed with the client or production partner before delivery.
