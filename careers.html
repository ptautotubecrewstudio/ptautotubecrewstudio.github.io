01Our Creative Culture

Different skills. One shared creative direction.

PT Autotube Crew Studio is a visual production studio working across 3D animation, CGI, VFX, cinematic production, advertising and digital content.

Our pre-to-post production approach connects creative development, production execution and finishing. Projects can involve a range of artistic and technical disciplines, depending on their scope and requirements.

We value clear communication, thoughtful creative problem-solving, professional standards and respect for the specialist contribution each collaborator brings to a project.

Important: This page introduces the disciplines relevant to our work. It does not advertise confirmed vacancies or guarantee employment, freelance assignments or project engagements.

03How Collaboration Works

A considered approach to working together.

Professional introductions are reviewed in context. The nature of any collaboration depends on the project, required expertise, timing and mutual agreement.

STEP 01

Introduce your work

Share your professional background, specialist discipline and a portfolio or selected examples of relevant work.

STEP 02

Discuss the fit

Where a relevant requirement exists, the next conversation can clarify experience, scope, responsibilities and availability.

STEP 03

Agree the details

Any employment or project engagement requires a separate, explicit agreement covering its applicable terms.

04Professional Introduction

Help us understand your contribution.

When contacting the studio, a clear introduction helps us understand your experience and the kind of work you do.

  • Your name and professional discipline.
  • A short overview of your experience and specialist skills.
  • A portfolio, showreel or selected work samples through a link you are comfortable sharing.
  • Your preferred type of collaboration and general availability, where relevant.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, confidential client material or sensitive personal data through a general enquiry.
