Build Your Future With PT Autotube Crew Studio

We are building a creative production team for CGI, 3D Animation, Visual Effects, Commercial Production and future animation projects. We welcome talented artists and production professionals who want to grow through meaningful creative work.

Work With Us

Our studio brings together creative and technical talent across different areas of digital production. We value strong portfolios, creative thinking, reliability, communication and a willingness to keep learning.

Creative Artists

Opportunities for artists working across 3D environments, modeling, texturing, animation, lighting, rendering and visual development.

Production Professionals

We also welcome professionals interested in production coordination, project management, client communication and creative operations.

Remote Collaboration

Depending on project requirements, we can collaborate with creative professionals remotely across different locations and time zones.

Creative Opportunities

The following areas represent the types of creative roles that may be relevant to our future production needs.

3D ART

3D Environment Artist

Create detailed environments for animation, CGI productions, cinematic content and interactive experiences.

  • Environment modeling
  • Texturing and materials
  • Lighting and composition
  • Strong portfolio required
3D ART

3D Generalist

Work across multiple stages of 3D production and contribute to complete visual content from asset creation to final output.

  • Modeling
  • Materials and texturing
  • Lighting and rendering
  • General production knowledge
ANIMATION

3D Animator

Create expressive and technically strong animation for cinematic, commercial and digital productions.

  • Character animation
  • Object animation
  • Timing and motion
  • Animation portfolio
VFX

VFX Artist

Support visual effects production through compositing, simulations, effects creation and integration.

  • Compositing
  • Visual effects
  • Simulation workflows
  • Problem solving
PRODUCTION

Production & Project Support

Help coordinate creative projects, production schedules, communication and delivery requirements.

  • Project coordination
  • Production tracking
  • Team communication
  • Client coordination
TECHNICAL

Technical Artist

Help connect artistic requirements with efficient technical workflows for modern 3D production.

  • Pipeline development
  • Optimization
  • Technical problem solving
  • Production tools
