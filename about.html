Studio Overview

Production Expertise Across Visual Media

PT Autotube Crew Studio is a Jakarta-based production house specialising in 3D Animation, CGI, VFX and cinematic content for film, gaming and advertising.

Our operations connect creative development, pre-production, production and post-production. Each project is approached according to its brief, scope, deliverables, schedule and technical requirements.

Based in Jakarta, Indonesia, the studio welcomes production enquiries and partnerships from local and international companies, studios, agencies and creative teams.
