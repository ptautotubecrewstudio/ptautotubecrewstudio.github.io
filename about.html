About PT Autotube Crew Studio

PT Autotube Crew Studio is a creative production studio based in Jakarta, Indonesia, focused on building high-quality digital visual content for brands, businesses, agencies and production partners.

Our work brings together creative thinking, 3D production and modern digital workflows to develop visual experiences for different production requirements and audiences.

Meet Our Team

Who We Are

A creative studio built around visual production, technical capability and long-term collaboration.

Creative Production With a Practical Approach

PT Autotube Crew Studio works across digital visual production with a focus on creating professional content that can support commercial, entertainment and communication objectives.

We combine artistic direction with production planning so that creative ideas can move through a structured process toward a usable final result.

Our studio is designed to support both individual projects and ongoing production relationships, depending on the needs of our clients and partners.

How We Work

Our production mindset is based on clear communication, organized execution and attention to the final visual result.

Understand

We begin by understanding the project's objectives, audience, visual requirements and expected deliverables.

Plan

Production requirements are organized into a practical workflow designed around the project's scope and timeline.

Create

Our creative and technical work is developed through the appropriate production stages for the project.

Deliver

The final work is prepared according to the agreed requirements and delivered in a professional production format.
