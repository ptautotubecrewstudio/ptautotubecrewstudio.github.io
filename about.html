About The Studio

Building Visual Worlds Through Creativity & Technology

PT Autotube Crew Studio is a creative production studio based in Jakarta, Indonesia, focused on CGI, 3D Animation, Visual Effects, Motion Graphics and digital visual production.

We combine creative thinking with modern production technology to develop visual content for brands, businesses, agencies and entertainment projects.

Who We Are

PT Autotube Crew Studio develops digital visual content using 3D, CGI, animation, visual effects and modern post-production workflows.

Technology

Modern Tools For Modern Production

Our workflow uses professional software and digital production technologies across modeling, animation, rendering, compositing, editing and finishing.

Depending on the project requirements, production may involve tools such as Blender, Unreal Engine, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Photoshop and Substance 3D Painter.

The technology is selected according to the creative and technical requirements of each project rather than following a single fixed workflow.
