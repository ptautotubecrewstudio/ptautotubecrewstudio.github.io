PT AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO

02. Personal Information We Collect

The type of information processed depends on how you interact with our website and company. We seek to limit the information requested to what is relevant to the specific interaction.

A. Contact Information

Full name, email address, company or organization name and other contact details that you voluntarily provide.

B. Enquiry Information

Enquiry category, project description, business requirements, messages, questions and communication preferences.

C. Project Information

Project references, creative briefs, production requirements, proposals and other relevant materials voluntarily shared during discussions.

D. Technical Information

Information that may be processed by the website hosting provider, such as IP address, browser details, requested page, access time and technical request information.

Unless specifically necessary and lawfully requested for a separate business process, please do not send sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card credentials, health records, identity documents or personal financial account details through general website enquiries.

04. How We Use Personal Information

Personal information may be processed for legitimate and specific business purposes, including:

  • Responding to questions, requests and business enquiries.
  • Evaluating potential projects, creative requirements and digital platform opportunities.
  • Preparing proposals, quotations and project documentation.
  • Communicating with prospective clients, customers, suppliers and business partners.
  • Managing contractual relationships and agreed production or development work.
  • Maintaining appropriate company records and meeting applicable legal obligations.
  • Protecting the website, communications and company systems against unauthorized access, misuse and security threats.
  • Handling privacy requests and resolving disputes where necessary.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, informing you or obtaining your consent.

05. Legal Basis for Processing

Under Indonesian personal data protection legislation, personal data processing must have an applicable legal basis.

Depending on the specific processing activity, the relevant basis may include:

  • Consent: Where you provide valid consent for a specific processing purpose.
  • Contractual Necessity: Where processing is necessary to perform a contract or take steps requested before entering into a contract.
  • Legal Obligations: Where processing is necessary to meet applicable legal requirements.
  • Legitimate Interests: Where permitted processing is necessary for legitimate interests, after considering your rights and interests.
  • Other Lawful Grounds: Where another legal basis expressly permitted under applicable Indonesian law applies.

The appropriate legal basis will depend on the particular purpose and circumstances of the processing.

06. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We do not make personal information publicly available or sell it as a business product. Information may be shared only where necessary for an appropriate business or legal purpose.

Potential Recipients

  • Authorized company personnel who need information to respond to enquiries or manage business activities.
  • Technology, hosting, communication and technical service providers where required to operate relevant services.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal representatives where appropriate.
  • Contractual production or project partners where sharing is necessary, authorized and appropriately documented.
  • Government authorities or other legally authorized recipients where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Confidentiality

We aim to limit access to personal information to authorized people and service providers who require it for the relevant purpose.

07. International Data Transfers

As a company operating in digital platforms and creative production, certain services used for website hosting, communications or business operations may involve service providers operating across different countries.

Where personal information is transferred outside Indonesia, the transfer must follow the applicable requirements of Indonesian personal data protection legislation.

This may require an assessment of the receiving country's data protection level, appropriate safeguards or another lawful basis permitted by applicable law.

We aim to ensure that appropriate protections are considered before relevant international processing arrangements are implemented.

08. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained, subject to applicable legal requirements.

General Retention Principles

  • Unsuccessful general enquiries should not be retained indefinitely.
  • Project-related records may be retained for the duration of the business relationship and any applicable legal retention period.
  • Financial, accounting and contractual records may be retained as required by applicable laws.
  • Technical hosting logs are subject to the relevant service provider's retention practices.
  • Information that is no longer necessary should be deleted, destroyed or anonymized where appropriate and legally permitted.

Retention administration: The company should maintain a documented retention schedule specifying the applicable time periods for different categories of information. Requests for deletion remain subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Requests will be assessed and handled according to applicable legal requirements. Certain requests may be restricted where a lawful exception or recordkeeping obligation applies.

10. Data Security & Protection

We aim to apply reasonable technical, organizational and administrative measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction or misuse.

Security Practices

  • Limiting access to information according to business responsibilities.
  • Using appropriate account and access controls.
  • Maintaining appropriate system and website security practices.
  • Reviewing relevant third-party services and business arrangements.
  • Managing security incidents and responding to potential data protection failures.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore encourage visitors not to send unnecessary confidential or sensitive information through general enquiries.

Data Protection Incidents

Where a personal data protection failure occurs, relevant investigations, containment, notifications and corrective actions will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

11. Cookies & Website Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on a visitor's browser. Websites may also use related technologies to support website functions, performance and user experience.

Current Website Configuration

The website's current source code does not include an installed advertising pixel or a website analytics tracking script. We will update this policy if additional tracking or measurement technologies are introduced.

Third-Party Fonts

Certain pages may request fonts from Google Fonts to display typography. When a browser requests these font resources, Google may receive technical request information such as the visitor's IP address and browser headers.

More information is available in Google's privacy documentation: Google Privacy Policy

Managing Cookies

Visitors can review their browser settings to manage or delete cookies. Blocking certain browser technologies may affect how some third-party services operate.
