Your Information

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be handled when you visit the PT Autotube Crew Studio website or contact the studio.

Last updated October 19 2026

About This Policy

PT Autotube Crew Studio operates this website to share information about the studio and its services. This policy describes the website setup currently in use and how information you choose to send may be handled.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses may be logged and stored for security. Please also read GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Information You Choose to Send

If you contact the studio you may choose to provide information such as your name email address company country phone or WhatsApp number project details and files or links you include. Please only send information you are allowed to share.

How Website Contact Works

The contact form currently opens your email application with a prepared message. The website itself does not upload or store that form information. The studio receives it only if you choose to send the email from your email application.

A WhatsApp link opens a conversation in WhatsApp. Information is shared with the studio only if you choose to send a message. Email and WhatsApp are operated by third parties and their own privacy terms apply.

Technical Information and Website Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. When you visit it GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the site. Read GitHub Pages information and GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Third-Party Services

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google to retrieve those fonts and Google may process technical request information under its Privacy Policy.

If you choose to use WhatsApp or an external link those services handle information under their own privacy policies. You can review WhatsApp's privacy policies before using WhatsApp.

This website does not currently send contact form responses to a spreadsheet or a website database. If a form service analytics tool or other data collection service is added the policy should be updated to explain that change.

How Information May Be Used

Information you send to the studio may be used to respond to your inquiry discuss a project provide agreed services maintain business records or address legal and security matters.

Sharing and Retention

The studio does not publish your private inquiry on this website. Information may be handled by the service providers needed to communicate with you or host the website. It may also be disclosed if required by applicable law.

Messages and project information are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond manage a business relationship maintain necessary records or meet legal obligations.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website pages described here do not intentionally use advertising cookies or an analytics service to collect contact form responses. GitHub Pages and third-party services may handle technical data according to their own policies. Your browser settings may let you manage cookies and external content.

Keeping Information Secure

The studio takes reasonable steps to handle received information responsibly. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Privacy Requests

Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection addresses the processing of personal data and the rights of individuals. You may contact the studio to ask about personal information you have sent or to request a correction or deletion where applicable.

Read the official Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law.

Children

This website is intended for general audiences and business inquiries. Please contact the studio if you believe a child has sent personal information through the website.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website features or information handling practices change. The latest update date will be shown near the start of this page.
