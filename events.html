01Why Events Matter

Creative work grows through shared knowledge.

Film, animation, CGI and visual effects are collaborative disciplines. Conversations between artists, producers, technical teams and creative partners help ideas move forward and make complex production challenges easier to understand.

This page is the studio's place to share confirmed event information and outline the kinds of creative exchanges that may be relevant to our field. It does not announce events that have not been scheduled.

02Areas of Creative Exchange

Conversations across the production world.

These are potential programme themes, not confirmed or scheduled events.

01 / FILM & PRODUCTION

Filmmaking and production conversations

Creative planning, production workflows, visual storytelling and collaboration across pre-production, production and post-production.

02 / ANIMATION & VFX

Animation, CGI and visual effects

Artistic direction, digital environments, character animation, compositing and the craft behind cinematic visual experiences.

03 / DIGITAL CREATIVITY

Digital creativity and technology

Creative tools, emerging production methods, digital content and the relationship between technology and visual communication.
