Transparency & Authenticity

Our Testimonial Publication Standards

Client feedback should represent genuine professional experiences and must not create misleading impressions.

Authentic Feedback. Authorized Publication.

AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO intends to maintain accuracy and transparency when presenting client testimonials and project-related endorsements.

Before publishing a testimonial, the relevant statement and supporting information should be verified.

  • Feedback must originate from a genuine client or authorized representative.
  • Client permission must be obtained before public attribution.
  • Names, job titles and company details must be accurately represented.
  • Client logos and photographs must not be used without appropriate authorization.
  • Testimonials must not be fabricated, misleadingly edited or presented outside their intended context.
  • Confidential project information must remain protected.

Where permission is limited, feedback may be presented anonymously only when the client has authorized that form of publication.
