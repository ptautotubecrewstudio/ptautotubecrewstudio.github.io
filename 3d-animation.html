3D ANIMATION

Digital Characters. Environments and Motion.

3D animation combines modelling, visual development, rigging, movement and rendering to create animated characters, objects and digital environments.

AUTOTUBE CREW STUDIO provides 3D animation capabilities for film, gaming, advertising and digital entertainment. Production requirements may include character performance, environmental animation, product sequences and cinematic visual storytelling.

Each assignment is developed according to its approved concept, visual references, animation requirements, production schedule and final deliverables.
