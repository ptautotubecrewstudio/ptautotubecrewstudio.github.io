Our Process

A Clear Production Workflow

Our production process is designed to keep creative goals, technical requirements and project communication aligned from beginning to final delivery.

01

Discovery & Brief

We begin by understanding the project, objectives, audience, references, technical requirements and expected deliverables.

02

Concept & Planning

The creative direction, visual references, production requirements and overall approach are organized before production begins.

03

Production

Our artists develop the required assets, animation, environments, visual effects, lighting and other production elements.

04

Review & Refinement

Project progress is reviewed and creative feedback is incorporated through the appropriate production stages.

05

Rendering & Post-Production

Final scenes are rendered and prepared through compositing, editing, color and other required finishing processes.

06

Final Delivery

Completed files are prepared according to the agreed technical specifications and delivery requirements.
