Our Workflow

How Our Creative Teams Work Together

A coordinated production process helps different creative disciplines stay aligned from the first idea to the final delivery.

01

Discover

The team reviews the project objectives, references, audience, creative direction and technical requirements.

02

Plan

Creative and technical requirements are organized into a practical production approach before work begins.

03

Create

Artists and production specialists develop the required designs, 3D assets, animation, effects and visual elements.

04

Review

Production is reviewed against the creative direction and project requirements so adjustments can be made when necessary.

05

Refine

Visual details, technical elements and finishing are refined to improve the overall quality of the production.

06

Deliver

The completed production is prepared according to the required technical specifications and delivery format.
