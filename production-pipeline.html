Production Pipeline

Every project follows a structured production pipeline to ensure creativity, technical excellence and high-quality final delivery.

Complete Production Process

01. Project Consultation

Understanding project goals, audience and creative requirements.

02. Research & Planning

Project planning, references, mood boards and production strategy.

03. Script Development

Writing the production script and defining the storytelling approach.

04. Storyboarding

Creating visual sequences and camera planning.

05. Concept Art

Designing the visual style, environments and assets.

06. Character & Environment Design

Developing production-ready concepts.

07. 3D Modeling

Creating optimized 3D models for production.

08. Sculpting & UV Mapping

Adding details and preparing assets for texturing.

09. Texturing & Materials

Applying realistic PBR textures and materials.

10. Rigging & Animation

Building rigs and producing smooth cinematic animation.

11. Lighting & Camera

Professional lighting and cinematic camera composition.

12. Visual Effects & Simulation

Fire, smoke, cloth, particles, water and advanced VFX.

13. Rendering

High-resolution rendering using industry-standard workflows.

14. Compositing & Color Grading

Combining render passes and enhancing the final look.

15. Sound Design & Video Editing

Final editing, music, sound effects and delivery preparation.

16. Quality Assurance & Final Delivery

Final review, client approval and delivery in the required formats.

Client Brief ↓ Planning ↓ Concept Art ↓ Modeling ↓ Texturing ↓ Rigging ↓ Animation ↓ Lighting ↓ Rendering ↓ Compositing ↓ Editing ↓ Final Delivery
